Founded in New Delhi, Red Panda Publications publishes a focused annual list of literary fiction, poetry and ghazals, narrative non-fiction, and Indian translations.

We believe digital tools should serve editorial craft rather than replace it. Our engineering team develops as an independent workbench for writers and small presses preparing print-ready books.
